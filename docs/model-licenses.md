## MiniMax H3

Under the [H3 Community License](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3/blob/main/LICENSE), the EU, UK, South Korea and US are excluded from the standard territorial grant. Deployment there requires separate authorization. Brazil is not excluded, but other conditions still apply.

These terms cover local weights and derivatives, including H3-based Omni variants and quantizations. Commercial products/services above US$20 million in yearly revenue require prior written authorization; commercial interfaces must prominently identify MiniMax H3.

Section V.4 also restricts displaying outputs outside the permitted territory. How this applies to globally accessible uploads remains unresolved in the [licensing discussion](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3/discussions/12); this app does not promise worldwide publication rights.

Sections V.2/V.5 require downstream terms and safeguards for products offering H3 generation. License notices alone do not establish compliance. Distribution/deployment needs review against the complete agreement and acceptable-use policy.
